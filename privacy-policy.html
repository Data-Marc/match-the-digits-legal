Summary

Effective date: October 21, 2026.

This policy explains what data is processed when you use the Match the Digits mobile application (the "App") on iOS and Android, why, by whom, for how long, and how to exercise your rights.

• The App does not ask you to create an account, and does not ask for your name, email address or date of birth to play.
• Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. We do not receive them.
• The App displays advertising provided by Google (AdMob). To do so, Google processes some data from your device, described in Section 5. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, your consent is requested before any personalized advertising.
• We only receive data about you if you write to us (support).

2. Data Stored Only on Your Device

The following data is stored in the App's local storage, on your device. It is not sent to us and is not sent to any server operated by the publisher:

• Game in progress (board, score, remaining hints and additions): to resume your game
• Best score: to show it on the home screen and in the trophies
• Daily results (boards cleared, points, pairs, daily challenges): calendar, statistics, trophies
• Preferences: language, haptic feedback, sound effects, reminders: to respect your settings
• Dates of the rating requests already shown: so that you are not asked too often to rate the App

This data is kept as long as the App is installed. You can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data (Android) or by uninstalling it (iOS and Android). It cannot be recovered afterwards: the publisher makes no backup.

3. Reminders (Notifications)

If you turn on reminders in the settings, the App asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device: no notification token is created or sent to a server. You can turn them off at any time in the App's settings or in your device settings. Reminders contain no advertising.

4. Support (When You Write to Us)

The "Contact support" button opens your email application with a pre-filled email addressed to hello@primisia.com. If you send it, we receive:

• your email address and the content of your message;
• the App version, the operating system (iOS or Android) and its version, added automatically at the bottom of the message to help us solve the problem. You can remove them before sending.

This data is used only to reply to you. It is deleted as soon as your request has been handled.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Data processed by Google

The App displays advertising provided by Google AdMob, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the App. According to the documentation Google publishes for developers, Google processes:

• IP address, which may be used to estimate the approximate location of the device: advertising, measurement, fraud prevention
• Interactions with the App (launches, taps, videos viewed): advertising, measurement, fraud prevention
• Diagnostic and performance data (launch time, hang rate, energy use); on iOS, crash logs not linked to the user: diagnostics, improvement, advertising, measurement
• Device identifiers: advertising ID (Android), App Set ID, other app- or developer-scoped identifiers: advertising and measurement
• Advertising data (ads viewed): advertising and measurement

Google states that this data is encrypted in transit (TLS). The publisher has no access to this individual data: it only receives aggregated statistics (number of impressions, revenue).

Ads shown in the App

• a banner on the game screen;
• an optional rewarded ad: you can choose to watch a video to get a hint, or to use the “+” button again (the first “+” of each board is free). Declining does not prevent you from playing.

If you have purchased "Remove ads" (Section 6), no ads are shown.

Your consent (EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

On first launch, a consent window managed by Google's consent tool lets you accept or refuse the use of your data for advertising purposes, purpose by purpose. If you refuse, the App remains fully playable; non-personalized or limited ads may then be shown. You can change your choice at any time in the App's settings.

iOS: tracking

The App does not ask for permission to track your activity (App Tracking Transparency): your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used. The App never requires you to accept tracking in order to play.

Android: advertising ID

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

United States

Depending on the State where you live, the law may give you the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your data for targeted advertising. You can exercise this choice from the App's privacy settings, or by writing to us.

6. "Remove ads" Purchase

The one-time purchase that removes ads is processed exclusively by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). We never receive your payment data (bank card, name, billing address). The App only receives confirmation from the store that the purchase was made, in order to remove the ads and allow it to be restored. Payment processing is governed by Apple's or Google's privacy policies.

7. Ratings and Reviews

When the App invites you to rate it, it uses Apple's or Google's official window. Your rating and review are processed by the store concerned, under its own rules; the App has no access to them.

8. Legal Bases (GDPR)

• Running the game and storing your progress and settings locally: performance of the service you request (contract)
• Access to device identifiers and advertising, personalized or not, where the law requires consent: consent, which you can withdraw at any time (Section 5)
• Showing reminders: consent (notification permission), which you can withdraw at any time
• Replying to your support requests: legitimate interest in replying to you; or pre-contractual measures / contract if the request concerns a purchase
• Compliance with legal obligations (for example, accounting obligations for purchases): legal obligation

9. Recipients and Transfers Outside the European Union

The data processed by the App is neither sold nor rented by the publisher. The only recipients are:

• Google, for advertising (Section 5);
• Apple or Google, for purchases, ratings and system notifications, depending on your device;
• the authorities, only where required by law.

Google may process data outside the European Union, in particular in the United States, under the safeguards described in its privacy policy.

10. Children

The App is intended for people aged at least 16. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data about them.

In France, a minor under 15 cannot consent alone to the processing of their data based on consent: the agreement of a holder of parental authority is also required (Article 45 of the French Data Protection Act, Loi Informatique et Libertés). If you are a parent and believe your child has given consent, write to us at hello@primisia.com: we will tell you how to withdraw it.

11. Your Rights

Depending on the applicable law (in particular the GDPR), you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and the right to withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.

• Data on your device: you control it directly (Section 2).
• Advertising consent: can be changed at any time from the App's settings (Section 5).
• Advertising data held by Google: tools and contacts described in Google's privacy policy.
• Support data: write to hello@primisia.com. We reply within one month, which may be extended under the conditions set out in the GDPR.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you may lodge a complaint with the CNIL (Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés), Service des plaintes, 3 place de Fontenoy, 75007 Paris, France, or online at https://www.cnil.fr/fr/adresser-une-plainte, or with the data protection authority of your country of residence.

12. Security

Game data stays on your device and benefits from its protections. Exchanges with Google are encrypted (TLS). No game data is stored on a server operated by the publisher.

13. Changes

We may change this policy, in particular if the App evolves. The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page. In the event of a significant change, you will be informed in the App before it applies.
