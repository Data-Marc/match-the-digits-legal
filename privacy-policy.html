Summary

Updated November 21, 2026.

You can play without providing a name, email or password. The leaderboard uses an anonymous account; a technical identifier does not make the associated data anonymous. Your game and settings stay on your device, while your leaderboard nickname and record are stored on our server and visible in the leaderboard. Third parties provide security, advertising and purchase services. You can request deletion of your leaderboard account, with or without erasing local progress.

2. Local progress and preferences

Your device stores your current game, local best score, daily challenge results, trophy statistics, preferences and rating-prompt dates. These support resuming play and displaying progress. The record intended for the leaderboard is transmitted separately as described below.

We do not offer a server backup that restores all your progress on another device. Clearing app data or uninstalling may lose that progress. Any operating-system backup depends on your device settings.

2a. Account and leaderboard

A technical anonymous account is created when you first access the leaderboard. Our self-hosted Supabase service stores the login identity, sessions, assigned nickname, published record, associated dates and account status. Session credentials are also kept on your device to recognize your account.

Your nickname, score and position are visible in the leaderboard; login credentials and deletion receipts are not public. An eligible record from a finished or abandoned game may be sent or retried later after a network failure. The leaderboard is not a backup of your game.

2b. Security and abuse prevention

We process technical request information, including the IP address at the service entry point, installation or network hashes and dates/counters, to protect the leaderboard, limit repeated registrations and handle deletions and restrictions. These hashes remain pseudonymous data.

Firebase App Check and Apple/Google attestation mechanisms verify app requests using technical data and attestation tokens. Firebase no longer provides leaderboard authentication or storage. Details: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

3. Reminders (Notifications)

If you turn on reminders in the settings, the App asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device: no notification token is created or sent to a server. You can turn them off at any time in the App's settings or in your device settings. Reminders contain no advertising.

4. Support

When you contact hello@primisia.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. The app-prepared message may include app and operating-system versions; you can remove them.

We use this information to respond, verify account-related requests and resolve issues. It is kept while handling the request, and afterwards only where needed for follow-up, legal claims or a legal obligation. Never send passwords, login tokens or banking details.

5. Advertising and privacy choices

Google AdMob supplies a banner and optional rewarded videos. Depending on the system and permissions, its SDK processes IP addresses, advertising or app identifiers, app/ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Premium removes ads, including rewarded videos.

Agreeing to watch a video is separate from consent to advertising data processing. Versions integrating Google UMP show a privacy form when the service requires it, before requesting ads; this form is configured for worldwide delivery. You can accept, refuse or manage the offered choices. In Settings, Ad privacy choices reopens the available options, including applicable US opt-outs from sale/sharing or targeted advertising. Refusing tracking does not prevent play. Google chooses the form language from the device settings and its supported translations; English may be used as a fallback. Older versions may not provide this entry: update the app or contact support to exercise your rights. Contacting support does not replace consent required before processing.

Android lets you reset or delete the advertising identifier in phone settings. On iOS, IDFA access and tracking subject to ATT require the corresponding Apple authorization. These settings alone do not erase data already held by providers. Local rights, including rights to opt out of certain targeted advertising, remain applicable.

Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Partner-service data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

6. Premium purchase and restoration

Apple or Google processes payment and retains billing information under its own rules. We do not receive your payment-card number.

RevenueCat helps verify and restore the one-time ad-free purchase. It processes a pseudonymous identifier, transaction/receipt information, product, store and Premium entitlement status. The app also stores information needed for Premium access. Deleting your leaderboard account does not delete this purchase; data needed for restoration and applicable obligations is handled separately.

Details: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

7. Ratings and Reviews

When the App invites you to rate it, it uses Apple's or Google's official window. Your rating and review are processed by the store concerned, under its own rules; the App has no access to them.

8. Purposes and legal bases

Gameplay, the requested leaderboard account and managing/restoring Premium rely on providing the service or contract. Security, anti-abuse limits and support rely on our legitimate interest in protecting the service and its users, subject to your rights. Reminders are optional and require device permission.

Advertising processing and identifier access rely on consent where required. Some retention is needed for legal obligations or establishing, exercising or defending legal claims. These bases do not authorize unrestricted reuse for other purposes.

10. Children

The App is intended for people aged at least 16. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data about them.

In France, a minor under 15 cannot consent alone to the processing of their data based on consent: the agreement of a holder of parental authority is also required (Article 45 of the French Data Protection Act, Loi Informatique et Libertés). If you are a parent and believe your child has given consent, write to us at hello@primisia.com: we will tell you how to withdraw it.

11. Account deletion and your rights

The leaderboard account section in Settings lets you request deletion and follow its progress. Once the server accepts the request, the profile is removed from the leaderboard and its identity and related active data are subsequently deleted. An offline request must first be transmitted; uninstalling alone does not delete the server account.

“Also erase my progress” resets the game, record and relevant local results. Without it, they remain on your device. Premium and preferences are preserved. After deletion completes, voluntarily rejoining creates a new identity and nickname, subject to anti-abuse restrictions. Your retained local record may be submitted again; the old account is not restored and its nickname may be reassigned.

Without app access, email hello@primisia.com, identifying Match the Digits and the leaderboard nickname/identifier you have, never a token or password. Proportionate verification may be necessary to avoid deleting someone else’s account.

Depending on your circumstances, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability and consent withdrawal. We normally respond within one month, extendable as permitted by law. You may complain to the CNIL at https://www.cnil.fr/fr/adresser-une-plainte or your competent national authority.

12. Security

Network exchanges use encryption in transit. Administrative access is restricted; backups and the decision journal are encrypted. These measures reduce risk without guaranteeing absolute security. The public leaderboard remains visible to its users; do not send secrets through support channels.

12a. Retention and backups

Local data remains until erased or replaced. The leaderboard account and record remain until the account is deleted. Temporary counters and security restrictions have their own expiry times; some traces are needed to prevent abuse and handle disputes.

Backups rotate through 7 daily, 4 weekly and 2 monthly restore points. This does not guarantee erasure of every copy on an exact date. The decision journal helps prevent a restore from reintroducing a deleted account.

Deletion receipts and this journal currently remain stored without automatic purging. They may contain technical identifiers, hashes, dates and status information. You may request a review of their retention or erasure at hello@primisia.com. Purchase data separately follows restoration needs and applicable obligations.

13. Policy changes
