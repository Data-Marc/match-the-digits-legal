Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?
No. Match the Digits requires no account, no registration and no login. You can start playing right after installing.
I bought "Remove ads" but I still see ads
Open the settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same App Store or Google Play account used for the original purchase.
Is "Remove ads" a subscription?
No. It is a one-time purchase: it is charged once and never renews. It removes all ads, including the optional rewarded ad for hints. It gives no gameplay advantage: every player has 4 hints per game.
How do hints work?
Each game gives you 4 hints. Without the "Remove ads" purchase, you can choose to watch a short ad to reveal a hint. Watching is optional: you can always keep playing without it.
How do I change my advertising choices?
Open the app's settings to review or change the consent you gave for advertising. On Android, you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
My reminder does not show up
Check that notifications are allowed for Match the Digits in your device settings, then confirm that reminders are turned on in the app's settings.
Will I lose my progress?
Your game, scores, daily results and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app, clearing its data or changing devices permanently deletes them: we keep no copy and cannot restore them.
How do I get a refund?
Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. Request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, or from Google following this procedure. You can also write to us.
